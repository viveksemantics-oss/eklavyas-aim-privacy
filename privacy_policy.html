Privacy Policy for Eklavya's Aim

Package Name: com.eklavya.aim • Last Updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Eklavya's Aim ("the App", developed by Vivek Kumar) handles information when you use our mobile puzzle game on Android or iOS.

1. Personal Information

Eklavya's Aim is an offline-first puzzle game. We do not require account registration and we do not directly collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location).

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your game progress, completed levels, best times, statistics, hint balance, and preferences (such as sound effects, haptics, and Eye Comfort theme) are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. You can clear this data at any time from the in-app Settings screen or by uninstalling the App.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display Banner, Interstitial, and optional Rewarded ads. Google AdMob and its mediation partners may automatically collect and process certain device and usage information—including the Android Advertising ID (AAID), IP address (for approximate region/country), device model, OS version, and ad interaction events—to serve and measure advertisements and prevent fraud.

For more details on how Google processes data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Where required by law (such as EEA/UK), the App requests your consent via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

4. In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (such as "Remove Advertisements") are processed securely by Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details.

5. Children's Privacy

Eklavya's Aim does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
